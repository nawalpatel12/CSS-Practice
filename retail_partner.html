Retail Partner App

Automating Downstream Sales activities and tracking seller activities

Core Functionality

The core of a 2025 partner app is built on self-service and real-time synchronization, allowing retailers to manage their business without constant manual intervention from sales reps.

HOW IT WORKS

Real-Time Inventory & Catalog Management

Retailers can view live stock levels across multiple warehouses and stores. This prevents "out-of-stock" frustrations and allows for back-in-stock alerts and visibility into item availability by specific location.

Intelligent Ordering & Reordering

Apps feature automated replenishment triggered when stock hits predefined thresholds. Advanced systems use AI for predictive replenishment, analyzing historical trends to suggest optimal order quantities.

B2B-Specific Checkout & Financing

Unlike B2C apps, partner apps support complex B2B payment methods, including buying on account, installment plans, and pay-by-loan. They also integrate CPQ (Configure, Price, Quote) tools for custom-configured products with variable pricing structures.

Omnichannel Integration

A unified experience where a retailer can research products on a desktop, place an order via the mobile app, and track fulfillment in real-time. This includes Click and Collect (BOPIS) for professional partners.

Advanced Tracking & Logistics

Integrated Order Management Systems (OMS) route orders to the most cost-effective warehouse. Retailers receive live GPS tracking for their deliveries and can manage returns or damaged goods directly through the app.

SECTION 8

Content Management Capabilities

Content is no longer just stored; it is strategically "activated" within the automation platform.

Content Management

Product Information Management (PIM)

Acts as a single source of truth for technical specifications, instruction manuals, and rich media assets such as high-definition videos and 3D product models.

Enablement

On-Demand Educational Assets

Provides retailers with access to whitepapers, webinars, and training modules, while AI-driven guided selling helps identify the best products through dynamic recommendations.

Personalization

Personalized Dashboards

Delivers tailored content based on retailer behavior, including custom promotions, loyalty point balances, and region-specific sales trends.

EFFICIENCY

AI-Generated Marketing Kits

Empowers retailers with AI-generated social media assets and pre-written product descriptions, enabling faster and more effective product marketing with minimal effort.
