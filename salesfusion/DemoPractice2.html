Case Study

DMS Implementation
for Rural Nepal

Sales Fusion offers a dedicated and customized solution called SubERP,
designed specifically to address the automation needs of rural sub-stockists.

The SubERP implementation has enabled greater visibility and streamlined
management of distribution chains deep within the rural areas of Nepal. This
ensures smoother operations, better tracking, and improved efficiency across the
network.

Sales Fusion provides end-to-end ownership of the entire implementation
process – from selecting the right solution, configuring it for specific needs, to
providing hands-on support to ensure tangible results.
